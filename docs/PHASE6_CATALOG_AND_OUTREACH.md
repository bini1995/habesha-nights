# Phase 6 — Catalog density and organizer outreach

Phase 6 turns the traction sprint into an executable operating queue. It adds 16 source-checked upcoming events—eight in NYC and eight in the DMV—so the September 1 catalog reaches 50 upcoming listings.

## What ships

- 16 approved events with current organizer or ticket sources
- 12 organizer records and 11 venue records
- eight NYC listings and eight DMV listings
- a date correction for the New York African Restaurant Week Festival, now listed for October 9–11
- a ten-organizer outreach queue in `docs/ORGANIZER_OUTREACH_PHASE6.csv`

The batch intentionally mixes Ethiopian-led music, Ethiopian and African diaspora venues, nightlife, festivals, culture, and professional gatherings. It does not include listings with unclear dates, stale ticket pages, or placeholder event details.

## First 48 hours after launch

1. Open `/admin/` and confirm the upcoming-event goal reads 50 of 50.
2. Work through the outreach CSV in priority order. Send each organizer only its own `source=organizer` listing link.
3. Record the date contacted and the reply in a private working copy of the CSV. Do not commit private contact details or conversation history.
4. Publish one NYC roundup and one DMV roundup using `source=instagram` links copied from the admin dashboard.
5. Share the strongest three listings through WhatsApp using `source=whatsapp` links.
6. Recheck events that occur within seven days before promoting them again.

## Organizer message

> Hey — I’m building Habesha Nights, a free discovery platform for Ethiopian, Eritrean, and adjacent diaspora events in NYC and the DMV. I added your upcoming event and send visitors directly to your official page: [EVENT LINK]. If you want to claim it, correct anything, or send future events, use “Claim this event” or “Submit an Event.” There is no charge during launch.

## What not to automate yet

Do not add bulk outreach, automated direct messages, paid placement checkout, or organizer accounts during this sprint. The next product decision should be based on replies, claims, submissions, views, and ticket clicks from these real listings.
